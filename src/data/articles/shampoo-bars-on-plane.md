## TSA Shampoo Bar Rules Summary

- **Carry-On (Cabin):** ✅ Allowed — any size, no restrictions, no liquids bag needed
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ Allowed — no restrictions
- **TSA Declaration Required:** No
- **Goes in quart-sized bag?:** No — solid items are exempt from the liquids bag requirement

---

## Are Shampoo Bars Considered a Liquid by TSA?

**No. Shampoo bars are solid and are NOT subject to the TSA 3-1-1 liquids rule.**

TSA classifies items as liquids based on whether they can flow, pour, spread, or spray. A solid shampoo bar does none of these — it is physically a solid and is treated the same as bar soap.

| Product Format | TSA Liquid Rule? | Carry-On Size Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Shampoo bar (solid)** | ❌ No | ✅ Any size |
| **Conditioner bar (solid)** | ❌ No | ✅ Any size |
| **Liquid shampoo (bottle)** | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ 3.4 oz max |
| **Liquid conditioner (bottle)** | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ 3.4 oz max |
| **Dry shampoo (aerosol spray)** | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ 3.4 oz max |
| **Dry shampoo (powder)** | ❌ No (powder) | ✅ Any size (over 12 oz may be screened) |

## How to Pack Shampoo Bars for Travel

### 1. Use a Soap Case or Tin
Shampoo bars need somewhere to dry and stay contained. Options:
- Metal tins with drainage holes
- Silicone soap pouches
- Bamboo soap dishes
- Reusable soap bags (felt or linen)

### 2. Let It Dry Before Packing
If you use the shampoo bar before packing, let it air dry for at least 30 minutes. A wet bar in a sealed case can get mushy over time.

### 3. Wrap in a Washcloth
A simple, compact option for travel — wrap the bar in a small washcloth to absorb any moisture and protect your bag.

### 4. Don't Put It in Your Liquids Bag
There's no need. Your shampoo bar does **not** belong in your quart-sized bag — it's not a liquid. Use that precious liquids bag space for sunscreen, lotion, or liquid makeup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I bring shampoo bars through TSA?**
A: Yes. Shampoo bars are solid and completely exempt from the TSA 3-1-1 liquids rule. Any size is allowed in carry-on bags.

**Q: Do shampoo bars need to go in the liquids bag?**
A: No. Only liquid-format products go in the quart-sized bag. Solid shampoo bars can go directly in your toiletry bag or anywhere in your carry-on.

**Q: What is the size limit for shampoo bars in carry-on?**
A: There is no size limit. Because they are solid, shampoo bars are completely unrestricted in carry-on bags.

**Q: Are conditioner bars also TSA-exempt?**
A: Yes. Conditioner bars, shampoo bars, and any solid-format hair care product are exempt from liquid rules and can be any size in carry-on.

**Q: Can I bring both a shampoo bar and liquid conditioner?**
A: Yes. The shampoo bar can be any size anywhere in your bag. The liquid conditioner must be 3.4 oz or less in your quart-sized bag.

**Q: Can I bring Lush shampoo bars on a plane?**
A: Yes. All Lush shampoo bars are solid and fully TSA-compliant. Any size, any quantity, in carry-on.
