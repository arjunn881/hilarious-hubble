TSA wait times vary enormously depending on the airport, terminal, time of day, and day of the week. At major hub airports like LAX, JFK, DFW, and IAH, security lines during peak hours can stretch to 45–90 minutes without TSA PreCheck. Understanding how to check live wait times — and how to avoid the worst lines — can save your trip.

---

## How to Check TSA Wait Times (Live)

### 1. TSA's Official Wait Time Tool
The **TSA official website** at [tsa.gov/what-to-expect/wait-times](https://www.tsa.gov/what-to-expect/wait-times) shows crowd-sourced, self-reported wait times submitted by travelers at each checkpoint. These are updated throughout the day.

### 2. MyTSA App (Official TSA Mobile App)
The **MyTSA app** (free, iOS and Android) shows:
- Current estimated wait times by airport and terminal
- Historical wait time patterns by hour and day
- What you can and can't bring through security
- Prohibited items checker

### 3. Airport-Specific Apps & Websites
Most major U.S. airports publish their own real-time security wait times:
- **LAX:** flylax.com → "Flights & Info" → "Security Wait Times"
- **JFK:** jfkairport.com → wait times by terminal
- **DFW:** dfwairport.com → airport maps with wait time indicators
- **IAH / George Bush Intercontinental:** fly2houston.com → live security wait data

### 4. Google Search
Searching **"[airport code] tsa wait times"** (e.g., "IAH TSA wait times" or "JFK TSA wait times") often surfaces real-time data cards directly in Google's search results, pulled from TSA's or the airport's data feeds.

### 5. Third-Party Apps
- **FlightAware** and **FlightRadar24** show gate/terminal information
- **Google Maps** sometimes shows crowd levels at airport terminals
- **iFly.com** aggregates security wait times and airport guides

## IAH TSA Wait Times (George Bush Intercontinental Airport, Houston)

**IAH** (George Bush Intercontinental Airport) is Houston's primary international hub and one of the busiest airports in the southern United States. It is served by **United Airlines** as a major hub.

### IAH Security Checkpoints

IAH has multiple terminals (A, B, C, D, E) each with their own TSA checkpoints. Wait times vary significantly by terminal:

| Terminal | Primary Airlines | Typical Peak Wait |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal A | United Airlines | 20–45 min |
| Terminal B | United Airlines | 20–40 min |
| Terminal C | United (international) | 30–60 min |
| Terminal D | International arrivals | 25–50 min |
| Terminal E | Domestic carriers | 15–35 min |

### IAH TSA PreCheck Wait Times
TSA PreCheck members at IAH typically wait **under 10 minutes** even during peak periods. PreCheck lanes are available in all terminals at IAH.

### Best Times to Go Through IAH Security
- **Lowest wait:** Weekday midday (10am–2pm), early morning (4:30–6am)
- **Highest wait:** Friday afternoons, Sunday evenings, holiday periods

## JFK TSA Wait Times (John F. Kennedy International Airport, New York)

**JFK** handles over 60 million passengers annually and operates across 8 terminals. Security wait times at JFK are among the highest in the U.S., particularly at Terminal 1 (international) and Terminal 4 (Delta/international hub).

### JFK Security Wait Times by Terminal

| Terminal | Primary Airlines | Typical Peak Wait |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal 1 | Air France, Lufthansa, JAL, Korean | 30–75 min |
| Terminal 2 | Delta | 20–45 min |
| Terminal 4 | Delta (main hub), international | 35–80 min |
| Terminal 5 | JetBlue | 20–45 min |
| Terminal 7 | British Airways, Finnair | 25–55 min |
| Terminal 8 | American Airlines | 25–55 min |

### JFK TSA PreCheck Wait Times
JFK is one of the highest-volume airports in the country. TSA PreCheck members typically wait **5–15 minutes** — far better than standard lanes during peak times.

> [!TIP]
> JFK Terminal 4 (Delta's international terminal) is consistently one of the busiest security checkpoints in the U.S. If departing from Terminal 4, arrive at least 2.5–3 hours early for international flights.

## DFW TSA Wait Times (Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport)

**DFW** is the second-busiest airport in the world by number of aircraft operations and the primary hub for **American Airlines**. It has 5 terminals (A, B, C, D, E) connected by the Skylink train.

### DFW TSA Checkpoint Wait Times

| Terminal | Primary Airlines | Typical Peak Wait |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal A | American Airlines | 20–45 min |
| Terminal B | American Airlines | 20–45 min |
| Terminal C | American Airlines | 25–50 min |
| Terminal D | International carriers | 30–65 min |
| Terminal E | American, low-cost carriers | 15–40 min |

### DFW Tips
- **TSA PreCheck lanes** are in all terminals at DFW and consistently save 20–40 minutes
- Morning rushes (6–9am) and evening surges (4–7pm) are the worst
- Terminal D (international) has the longest waits for customs and immigration on arrival
- DFW's layout means you may need to ride the Skylink to reach your gate after security

## Factors That Affect TSA Wait Times

Several variables consistently drive longer or shorter security lines:

| Factor | Impact on Wait Times |
|---|---|
| Time of day | Early morning (5–7am) and midday are fastest |
| Day of week | Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, Sunday evenings are slowest |
| Holidays | Thanksgiving, Christmas/New Year, July 4th see 2–3× normal wait times |
| TSA PreCheck | Cuts wait to under 10 min at most airports |
| Terminal/airline | Hub terminals for major carriers always busier |
| Staffing | TSA staffing shortages extend wait times systemwide |

## How to Beat Long TSA Lines: Tips

1. **Get TSA PreCheck** — The single most impactful change you can make. $78 for 5 years.
2. **Arrive early** — For domestic flights: 1.5–2 hours. International: 2.5–3 hours.
3. **Check wait times before you leave** — Use the MyTSA app or airport website.
4. **Travel during off-peak hours** — Midday Tuesday/Wednesday flights see the shortest security lines.
5. **Pre-pack your liquids bag** — Having it ready to pull out instantly speeds up your lane.
6. **Wear easy shoes** — Slip-on shoes eliminate fumbling if you're in a standard lane.
7. **Use Clear (biometric screening)** — CLEAR uses fingerprint/iris scanning to confirm identity quickly, then routes you to a TSA officer (not the same as PreCheck, but can help at busy airports).

## ATL TSA Wait Times (Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport)

**ATL** (Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta) is consistently ranked the **world's busiest airport by passenger count**. It is the primary hub for **Delta Air Lines** and has two main security checkpoints (North and South).

### ATL Security Layout

ATL has two main security checkpoints that feed into the same underground concourse train system:

| Checkpoint | Location | Typical Peak Wait |
|---|---|---|
| **North Security (N)** | Main domestic terminal, north side | 20–50 min |
| **South Security (S)** | Main domestic terminal, south side | 20–45 min |
| **International Terminal (F)** | Separate building | 30–60 min |

### ATL Tips
- Read our full checkpoint breakdown in the [ATL TSA Wait Times Guide](/guide/atl-tsa-wait-times)
- N and S checkpoints process the same concourses — use whichever has a shorter line
- International Terminal F has its own security and is entirely separate from the domestic terminal
- ATL is one of the best airports for TSA PreCheck — the dedicated South Checkpoint serves PreCheck and Digital ID
- Check [atl.com](https://www.atl.com/) for live ATL security wait data
