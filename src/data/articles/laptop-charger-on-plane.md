One of the most common questions travelers ask when packing electronics is: **Can I put my laptop charger in checked luggage, or must it be carried on the plane?**

The direct answer: **Yes! You can put your laptop charger in checked luggage or in your carry-on bag.** Standard laptop chargers, cords, and charging bricks do not contain lithium batteries and are 100% allowed in checked baggage on every airline worldwide.

---

## Quick Rule Summary

- **Carry-On Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED**
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED**
- **Lithium Battery Status:** ❌ **NO Lithium Cells** (Standard wall power adapters do not store power)
- **Power Banks (Portable Chargers):** ⚠️ **CARRY-ON ONLY** (External battery packs must NOT be checked)

---

## Why People Get Confused: Chargers vs. Power Banks

Travelers often confuse **laptop chargers** (wall adapters) with **power banks** (portable battery packs):

| Device Type | What It Does | Contains Lithium Battery? | Carry-On Baggage | Checked Baggage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Laptop Charger / Power Brick** | Plugs into wall outlet to power/charge your laptop | ❌ **NO** | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Power Bank / Portable Battery** | Stores rechargeable energy to charge devices on the go | ✅ **YES** | ✅ Allowed (<100Wh) | ❌ **PROHIBITED** |
| **Laptop Itself** | Computer containing internal lithium battery | ✅ **YES** | ✅ Allowed (Recommended) | ⚠️ Allowed if powered off (Not recommended) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Golden Rule of Packing Electronics:**  
> If an electronic device or accessory **plugs into a wall outlet to draw electricity and has no internal rechargeable battery**, it is safe and allowed in checked luggage. Only devices that **store energy in loose or standalone lithium-ion batteries** must stay in your cabin baggage.

---

## TSA & Airline Regulations for Laptop Chargers

1. **FAA & IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations:** Wall charging bricks, USB-C power delivery chargers, MagSafe adapters, and standard power cables do not present any thermal runaway or fire hazards and have no restrictions.
2. **TSA Security Screening:** If you pack your laptop charger in your carry-on bag, you do not need to take the charger out at the security checkpoint. Only laptops and tablets themselves need to be placed in separate bins (unless using TSA PreCheck).
3. **Using Laptop Chargers on Planes:** Many commercial airliners offer in-seat AC power outlets (110V/60Hz). You can keep your charger in your personal backpack and use it during your flight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pack my laptop charger in my checked suitcase?
Yes. Laptop chargers, power cords, and wall plugs can be packed inside checked suitcases without any airline restriction or declaration.

### Do laptop chargers have lithium batteries?
No. Standard laptop chargers (such as MacBook chargers, Dell power bricks, and Lenovo chargers) contain transformers, capacitors, and circuitry to convert AC wall power to DC power, but they contain zero battery cells.

### Can I check in my iPad or tablet?
While you can technically check tablets if they are completely powered off, the FAA and airlines strongly advise keeping all iPads, tablets, and laptops in your carry-on luggage to prevent transit damage, theft, and battery safety risks.
