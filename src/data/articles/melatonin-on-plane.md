Melatonin is the go-to supplement for combating jet lag on long-haul flights. However, because melatonin regulations vary dramatically around the world, travelers often wonder: **Can you bring melatonin on a plane, and is it legal to take through airport security and customs?**

Here is the direct answer: **Melatonin tablets and gummies are 100% permitted in carry-on and checked luggage on domestic US flights and international departures.** When arriving in international destinations like the UK, France, and Singapore, personal travel quantities (up to a 3-month supply) are permitted under specific guidelines.

---

## Quick Rule Summary

- **Carry-On Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED** (Tablets, capsules, and solid gummies)
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED**
- **TSA Screening:** No declaration required for solid pills/gummies
- **Liquid Melatonin Drops:** ⚠️ Allowed in carry-on if ≤ 3.4 oz (100 ml) or declared as medically necessary liquid
- **UK Rules (MHRA / CAA):** ✅ Up to 3-month personal supply allowed
- **France Rules:** ✅ OTC up to 1.9mg allowed; 3-month personal supply permitted
- **Singapore Rules (HSA):** ✅ Allowed as health supplement (up to 3 months)

---

## TSA Airport Security Screening

1. **Solid Pills & Gummies:** Solid melatonin tablets, chewables, and gummies are treated as solid vitamins. They do not count toward your 3-1-1 liquid bag limit.
2. **Medication in Ziploc Bags:** TSA does not require medications or supplements to be in original labeled bottles for domestic flights. You can organize your melatonin pills into pill organizers or Ziploc baggies.
3. **Liquid Drops:** Melatonin tinctures or liquid sleep drops must be in bottles of 3.4 oz (100 ml) or less to fit in your quart liquid bag, unless declared separately as a medically necessary liquid.

## Country-by-Country International Customs Rules

International travelers must understand how foreign customs treat melatonin upon arrival:

### United Kingdom (UK Border Force & MHRA)
- **Status:** **Prescription-only to buy locally, but legal for incoming travelers.**
- **The Rule:** In the UK, melatonin is classified as a prescription-only medicine (POM). However, the UK Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) and Border Force explicitly allow international travelers to bring up to a **3-month personal supply** into the country.
- **Tip:** Keep your melatonin in its original manufacturer container to ensure smooth customs clearance.

### France (DGAC & French Customs)
- **Status:** **Legal over-the-counter under 1.9mg.**
- **The Rule:** France allows non-prescription sales of melatonin food supplements containing up to 1.9mg per daily dose. Higher therapeutic doses (over 2mg) are treated as pharmaceutical products. Incoming travelers can bring standard personal supplies (up to 3 months).

### Singapore (Health Sciences Authority - HSA)
- **Status:** **Legal as a Health Supplement.**
- **The Rule:** The Singapore HSA classifies melatonin as a health supplement. Travelers arriving at Changi Airport may bring up to a **3-month personal supply** without requiring an import license or advance HSA notification.

### Australia (TGA & Australian Border Force)
- **Status:** **Legal for personal travel.**
- **The Rule:** Melatonin is available by prescription (and OTC for adults 55+ in pharmacies). Travelers may import up to a 3-month supply under the TGA Personal Importation Scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you bring melatonin gummies on a plane?
Yes! Melatonin gummies are classified as solid food/supplements. They are allowed in unlimited reasonable quantities in both carry-on bags and checked suitcases with zero liquid restrictions.

### Do I need a prescription to bring melatonin into the UK?
No. International visitors bringing their own personal melatonin supply (up to 3 months) into the UK for personal travel do not need a UK doctor's prescription.

### Can I put melatonin in my personal item bag?
Yes. It is recommended to keep melatonin and all essential sleep aids in your personal item backpack so they are accessible during long-haul flights.
