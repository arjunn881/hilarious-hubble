One of the most searched questions before any flight: **can you bring both a carry-on bag AND a personal item?** The short answer is yes — almost every airline allows one of each. But the size rules, what counts as a personal item, and how strictly they're enforced vary significantly by airline.

---

## Carry-On + Personal Item: Quick Summary

- **Allowed on most airlines:** ✅ Yes — 1 carry-on + 1 personal item
- **Personal item goes:** Under the seat in front of you
- **Carry-on bag goes:** In the overhead bin
- **TSA involvement:** TSA does not regulate carry-on counts — airlines do
- **Exceptions:** Basic Economy fares on some airlines (American, United, Delta) restrict you to personal item only

---

## What Is a Personal Item?

A **personal item** is a smaller bag that must fit under the seat in front of you. Common personal items include:

| Item | Counts as Personal Item? |
|---|---|
| Small backpack (under seat size) | ✅ Yes |
| Laptop bag or sleeve | ✅ Yes |
| Purse or handbag | ✅ Yes |
| Tote bag | ✅ Yes |
| Briefcase | ✅ Yes |
| Camera bag | ✅ Yes |
| Diaper bag | ✅ Yes (usually free even on Basic Economy) |
| Duffel bag (small) | ✅ Yes (if it fits under seat) |
| Rolling carry-on suitcase | ❌ No — that's a carry-on, not a personal item |
| Full-size backpack | ❌ No — usually too large for personal item |

## Basic Economy: When You Can Only Bring a Personal Item

This is the most important exception. **Basic Economy fares** on the three major U.S. carriers restrict carry-on bags:

| Airline | Basic Economy Carry-On Rule |
|---|---|
| **American Airlines Basic Economy** | Personal item only. No overhead carry-on unless you have status or co-brand credit card |
| **United Airlines Basic Economy** | Personal item only. No overhead carry-on |
| **Delta Basic Economy** | Personal item only on most routes. No overhead carry-on |

> [!WARNING]
> If you book Basic Economy and show up with a carry-on bag expecting overhead bin space, you will be required to check it and pay the checked bag fee (typically $35–$65) at the gate.

**Southwest Airlines does NOT have Basic Economy** — all Southwest fares include one carry-on + one personal item + two free checked bags.

## Can You Bring Both a Carry-On and Personal Item on International Flights?

Yes — **international flights follow the same one carry-on + one personal item rule**, but weight limits are more commonly enforced by international carriers:

- **European carriers** (Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz Air) are particularly strict — often your personal item IS your only free bag, and carry-ons require purchasing a "priority" fare add-on
- **Middle Eastern carriers** (Emirates, Qatar, Etihad) allow one carry-on + one personal item with generous weight limits (7–10 kg)
- **Asian carriers** (Singapore Airlines, ANA, JAL) typically allow 7–10 kg carry-on + personal item

## Tips to Maximize Your Carry-On + Personal Item Allowance

1. **Pack your personal item last** — fill your carry-on first, then use the personal item for overflow, valuables, and in-flight essentials
2. **Use a structured personal item** — a laptop backpack or organized tote holds far more than a slouchy bag
3. **Know your fare class** — always check if your ticket includes overhead bin access before packing
4. **Wear your heavy items** — jacket, boots, and heavy sweaters don't count toward bag limits when worn
5. **Use packing cubes** — compress clothing in your carry-on to maximize space for your personal item to hold overflow
6. **Pack compliance-sensitive items securely** — pack your [electric toothbrush](/guide/electric-toothbrush-on-plane), [protein powder](/guide/protein-powder-on-plane), and [quart-sized liquids bag](/guide/tsa-311-liquids-rule) at the top of your bag for fast checkpoint access (or get [TSA PreCheck](/guide/tsa-precheck-guide) to avoid unpacking altogether).

## What If I Have More Than 2 Bags?

If you have more than one carry-on and one personal item:
- You must **check the extra bags** (fees apply — typically $30–$65 per bag)
- Gate agents will ask you to check overflow bags if overhead bins are full
- **Do not try to bring two carry-on bags** — gate agents actively count and size-check bags

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can you bring a carry-on and a personal item on every flight?**
A: On standard fares, yes. On Basic Economy fares with American, United, or Delta, you are limited to a personal item only unless you have elite status or a co-brand credit card.

**Q: What counts as a personal item?**
A: A small backpack, purse, laptop bag, briefcase, camera bag, or tote that fits under the seat in front of you. The exact size varies by airline but is typically around 18" x 14" x 8" or smaller.

**Q: Can I bring a personal item AND a carry-on for free?**
A: On most airlines with standard fares, yes — both are included in your ticket price. On ultra-low-cost carriers like Spirit and Frontier, carry-on bags cost extra (personal items are free).

**Q: Does a purse count as a personal item?**
A: Yes. A purse is the quintessential personal item. As long as it fits under the seat, it counts as your personal item.

**Q: Can I use my personal item as a second carry-on in the overhead bin?**
A: No. Personal items must go under the seat. If you put your personal item in the overhead bin, a flight attendant will likely ask you to move it.

**Q: What if I have a medical bag or baby diaper bag?**
A: Medical bags and diaper bags are typically allowed as a free additional item on top of your carry-on and personal item. Inform the gate agent if you are traveling with a medical device bag.

## Conclusion

The standard rule across virtually every airline is **one carry-on bag + one personal item per passenger**. The carry-on goes overhead; the personal item goes under the seat. The biggest exception is Basic Economy fares, which restrict you to a personal item only. Always check your specific fare class and airline rules before you pack — especially on ultra-low-cost carriers where carry-on bags cost extra.
