Nicotine pouches like ZYN, Velo, On!, and Rogue are popular alternatives to smoking and vaping when traveling. But can you bring nicotine pouches on an airplane, and are there international restrictions?

The short answer: **Nicotine pouches are 100% allowed on domestic US flights and most European routes in both carry-on and checked bags.** However, **they are strictly banned in Singapore (including transit at Changi Airport)** and regulated in select international destinations.

---

## Quick Rule Summary

- **Carry-On Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED** (Solid item, zero 3-1-1 liquid restrictions)
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ **ALLOWED** (Permitted in hold luggage)
- **TSA Rules:** ✅ Allowed without declaration on US flights
- **Singapore / Changi Airport:** ❌ **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** (Up to SGD $2,000 fine)
- **Using On Board:** ⚠️ Permitted discreetly on most airlines (no vapor or smoke)

---

## TSA & Domestic Flight Regulations

Nicotine pouches are solid, dry (or lightly moist) consumer products. Because they contain no liquids, lithium batteries, or explosive components:

1. **No 3-1-1 Liquid Restrictions:** Pouches do not count towards your 3.4 oz (100 ml) liquid bag. You can pack as many cans as you need for personal travel.
2. **Security Screening:** Keep cans in your carry-on or personal backpack. You do not need to remove them from your bag at TSA security checkpoints.
3. **Using Them On the Plane:** Unlike smoking and vaping (which are strictly illegal on all flights), oral nicotine pouches produce no smoke, vapor, or lingering odor. Most airlines permit discreet oral pouch use during flight.

## Critical International Regulations: Singapore Changi Airport Ban

> [!WARNING]
> **Singapore Ban on Nicotine Pouches & Vapes**  
> Under Singapore's *Tobacco (Control of Advertisements and Sale) Act*, all tobacco-free nicotine pouches, oral snus, and e-cigarettes are **completely illegal to import, possess, or purchase in Singapore**.
> 
> - **Fine:** Up to SGD $2,000 for possession or import at Singapore Changi Airport.
> - **Transit Passengers:** If you have to clear immigration or change terminals with baggage re-check, your bags are subject to Singapore Customs scanning. Do not bring nicotine pouches into Singapore.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you bring ZYN in your carry-on bag?
Yes! ZYN and all solid nicotine pouches are allowed in carry-on and personal item bags without quantity restrictions on US domestic and European flights.

### Can you pack nicotine pouches in checked luggage?
Yes, nicotine pouches contain no lithium batteries or fire hazards, so they can be safely packed in checked suitcases.

### What happens if you bring nicotine pouches to Singapore airport?
Singapore Customs and airport security at Changi Airport will confiscate the pouches and issue on-the-spot fines up to SGD $2,000 for possession of banned tobacco analogues.
