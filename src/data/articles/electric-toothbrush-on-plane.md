Electric toothbrushes are one of the most travel-friendly personal care devices you own — and the good news is TSA has very permissive rules about them. Here's everything you need to know about flying with an electric toothbrush.

---

## TSA Electric Toothbrush Rules Summary

- **Carry-On (Cabin):** ✅ Allowed — no restrictions
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ Allowed — with one important note about lithium batteries
- **TSA Declaration Required:** No
- **Battery rule:** Built-in rechargeable lithium batteries → carry-on preferred; AA/AAA batteries → either bag fine

---

## Are Electric Toothbrushes Allowed on Planes?

**Yes — electric toothbrushes are fully allowed in both carry-on and checked bags.** TSA has no prohibition on electric toothbrushes. They are treated like any other personal care appliance (similar to electric razors and hair dryers).

---

## Battery Type Matters: Carry-On vs Checked Bag

The key variable is your toothbrush's battery type:

| Battery Type | Carry-On | Checked Bag |
|---|---|---|
| **Built-in rechargeable lithium battery** (Oral-B, Philips Sonicare) | ✅ Recommended | ⚠️ Allowed, but carry-on preferred |
| **Removable AA or AAA batteries** | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **No battery (plug-in only)** | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |

### Why Lithium Battery Toothbrushes Are Better in Carry-On

TSA and FAA recommend that **devices with non-removable lithium batteries be carried in your carry-on** rather than checked baggage. This is because lithium batteries can be a fire risk if damaged, and cabin crew can respond to issues during flight — checked bag fires are much harder to address.

For premium electric toothbrushes like Oral-B iO, Philips Sonicare DiamondClean, and Waterpik Sonic Fusion — all of which have built-in lithium batteries — **carry-on is the safest and TSA-preferred option**.

> [!NOTE]
> While checked baggage is technically allowed for most consumer lithium battery devices, you protect yourself by keeping your electric toothbrush in your carry-on. It also prevents potential damage from checked bag handling.

## Ryanair and Low-Cost European Carrier Rules

Ryanair, easyJet, and other European low-cost carriers follow EASA (European Union Aviation Safety Agency) rules, which align with TSA rules for electric toothbrushes:

- **Ryanair:** Electric toothbrushes are allowed in carry-on and checked baggage
- **easyJet:** Electric toothbrushes are allowed in both bags
- **Wizz Air:** Electric toothbrushes are allowed in both bags

The same lithium battery guidance applies on European flights — carry-on is preferred for rechargeable battery devices.

---

## Do I Need to Remove the Battery?

For AA/AAA battery toothbrushes going in checked bags, TSA recommends:
- **Remove batteries** from devices in checked bags to prevent accidental activation
- This is especially important for toothbrushes with a physical on/off switch that could turn on in transit

For built-in lithium battery toothbrushes, you cannot remove the battery — just pack them in carry-on.

---

## Packing Tips for Electric Toothbrushes

1. **Use a toothbrush travel case** — Most premium electric toothbrushes come with a travel case or cover for the brush head. Use it. Loose brush heads can collect bacteria and damage the bristles.
2. **Remove the brush head** — Store the brush head separately from the handle in your toiletry bag to save space and protect bristles.
3. **Bring only the charger you need** — Most Oral-B and Sonicare toothbrushes have a 2-week+ battery life. For short trips, you may not need the charging stand at all.
4. **International charging** — Check your charger's voltage range. Most modern electric toothbrush chargers support 100–240V (universal), but verify before your trip. You may need a plug adapter (not a voltage converter) for foreign outlets.
5. **Pack in carry-on** — For lithium battery models, always carry-on to protect the battery and comply with TSA best practices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I bring an electric toothbrush in my carry-on bag?**
A: Yes. Electric toothbrushes are fully allowed in carry-on bags with no TSA restrictions.

**Q: Can I pack my Oral-B or Sonicare in my checked bag?**
A: Yes, technically. However, since these use built-in lithium batteries, carry-on is recommended by TSA and FAA. Checked bag is allowed but not ideal for lithium battery devices.

**Q: Do I need to remove the battery from my electric toothbrush?**
A: For removable AA/AAA battery models in checked bags — yes, recommended. For built-in lithium battery models — you can't remove it; just pack in carry-on.

**Q: Can I bring my Oral-B charging dock/stand on a plane?**
A: Yes. Charging docks and stands have no restrictions in carry-on or checked baggage.

**Q: Will my electric toothbrush go off in my bag during the flight?**
A: It shouldn't if properly switched off. For battery models in checked bags, remove the batteries to prevent accidental activation.

**Q: Can I bring multiple electric toothbrushes?**
A: Yes. There is no limit on the number of electric toothbrushes you can bring.
