Airport identity verification is evolving rapidly. TSA has introduced two closely related programs — **TSA Touchless ID** and **TSA Confirm ID** — as part of its broader modernization of security checkpoints. Both use biometric technology to speed up and improve the reliability of traveler identity checks.

---

## TSA Confirm ID & Touchless ID: Summary

| Feature | TSA Touchless ID | TSA Confirm ID |
|---|---|---|
| **What it does** | Biometric facial recognition at checkpoint | Identity verification for TSA PreCheck enrollment |
| **Who uses it** | TSA PreCheck members at participating airports | Travelers enrolling in or renewing TSA PreCheck |
| **Technology** | Facial recognition cameras at TSA lanes | Secure online identity proofing |
| **Required?** | Optional (you can opt out) | Required for PreCheck enrollment |
| **Run by** | TSA | IDEMIA (TSA-authorized vendor) |

## What Is TSA Touchless ID?

**TSA Touchless ID** is a biometric facial recognition system deployed at select airport security checkpoints. Instead of manually handing your ID to a TSA officer for visual inspection, a camera captures your face and matches it against your passport or driver's license photo stored in government databases.

### How TSA Touchless ID Works

1. You approach the TSA document check station
2. A camera captures your face automatically
3. The system compares your face to your government-issued ID photo
4. Verification is confirmed in seconds — the officer sees a green check
5. You proceed normally through the security lane

**TSA Touchless ID is currently available at:**
- Select lanes at major hub airports (including LAX, JFK, DFW, IAH, and others)
- TSA PreCheck dedicated lanes at participating airports

### Can I Opt Out of TSA Touchless ID?

Yes. TSA Touchless ID / facial recognition is **optional**. You may decline the biometric scan and have your ID manually checked by the TSA officer. Simply tell the officer you prefer manual ID verification.

> [!NOTE]
> Opting out of facial recognition does not affect your TSA PreCheck status. You will still be processed through the PreCheck lane; the officer will verify your ID manually.

## What Is TSA Confirm ID?

**TSA Confirm ID** refers to the identity verification step in the TSA PreCheck enrollment and renewal process — particularly when done through IDEMIA's online portal.

### IDEMIA and TSA PreCheck Identity Verification

**IDEMIA** is one of two companies authorized by TSA to enroll travelers in TSA PreCheck. IDEMIA's enrollment process includes:

1. **Online Application:** Complete your PreCheck application at IDEMIA's enrollment site (identogo.com)
2. **Identity Confirmation:** You confirm your identity through document upload and a liveness check (selfie) OR at an in-person appointment
3. **Fingerprinting:** A TSA-required fingerprint scan at the enrollment center
4. **Background Check:** TSA runs a security background check
5. **KTN Issued:** Your Known Traveler Number is sent by TSA

The "TSA confirm ID" step is the identity proofing stage where IDEMIA verifies your documents are genuine and match your person — preventing fraud in the PreCheck enrollment process.

## IDEMIA TSA PreCheck: How It Works

IDEMIA is the largest authorized TSA PreCheck enrollment provider, operating thousands of locations across the United States including:

- **Airports** (pre-security zone)
- **UPS Stores**
- **AAA locations**
- **Standalone enrollment centers**

### Booking a TSA PreCheck Appointment via IDEMIA

1. Go to [identogo.com](https://www.identogo.com/)
2. Search for enrollment locations near you
3. Select a date and time slot
4. Attend your appointment with:
   - Valid photo ID (passport or REAL ID driver's license)
   - Proof of U.S. citizenship (passport, birth certificate, or naturalization certificate)
5. Complete fingerprinting and identity confirmation
6. Pay the $78 enrollment fee

IDEMIA submits your biometric data to TSA, which runs the background check and issues your KTN.

## TSA Touchless ID vs. TSA PreCheck Biometric Enrollment: What's the Difference?

These are two different programs that both use biometric technology:

| | **TSA PreCheck Biometric Enrollment** (IDEMIA) | **TSA Touchless ID** (Checkpoint) |
|---|---|---|
| **When it happens** | Before your trip, at enrollment | At airport security checkpoint |
| **Purpose** | Verify identity to enroll in PreCheck | Speed up document check at security |
| **Technology** | Fingerprints + photo ID verification | Facial recognition camera |
| **Required?** | Yes (to join PreCheck) | Optional (can opt out) |
| **Who stores data** | TSA / IDEMIA (encrypted) | TSA (biometric deleted after use, per TSA policy) |

## What Documents Do I Need to Confirm My Identity at TSA?

At a standard TSA checkpoint (without PreCheck), you must present:
- A **REAL ID-compliant** state driver's license or ID card
- OR a **U.S. passport or passport card**
- OR another TSA-accepted form of ID (military ID, permanent resident card, etc.)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> As of May 7, 2025, TSA requires REAL ID-compliant identification for domestic air travel. Standard driver's licenses without the REAL ID star marking are no longer accepted at security checkpoints.

### Alternative ID Options (TSA Confirm ID Process)

If you don't have a REAL ID, you can still fly using:
- U.S. Passport
- U.S. Passport Card
- DHS Trusted Traveler Card (Global Entry, NEXUS, SENTRI, FAST)
- U.S. Military ID
- Permanent Resident Card (Green Card)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is TSA Confirm ID?**
A: TSA Confirm ID refers to the identity verification process in TSA PreCheck enrollment — the step where IDEMIA or another authorized enrollment provider confirms your identity through document verification and biometric collection.

**Q: What is TSA Touchless ID?**
A: TSA Touchless ID is an optional biometric facial recognition system at airport security checkpoints. Instead of handing your ID to an officer, a camera matches your face to your ID photo automatically.

**Q: What is IDEMIA TSA PreCheck?**
A: IDEMIA is a company authorized by TSA to process PreCheck enrollments. They handle in-person fingerprinting, identity verification, and submit your application to TSA. You can book enrollment appointments at identogo.com.

**Q: Can I opt out of TSA Touchless ID?**
A: Yes. Facial recognition at TSA checkpoints is voluntary. Tell the TSA officer you prefer manual ID verification and they will check your ID manually. Your PreCheck status is not affected.

**Q: Do I need a REAL ID to use TSA PreCheck?**
A: TSA PreCheck members use their Known Traveler Number (KTN) on their boarding pass to access PreCheck lanes. You still need a valid, accepted ID (REAL ID or passport) to board your flight.

## Conclusion

TSA Confirm ID and TSA Touchless ID are both part of TSA's effort to modernize airport security. The enrollment identity verification (run by IDEMIA) ensures TSA PreCheck members are thoroughly vetted before receiving expedited screening benefits. The optional touchless facial recognition at checkpoints further speeds up the process for travelers who opt in. Both systems use encrypted biometric data with strong privacy controls — and you can always opt out of checkpoint facial recognition if you prefer manual verification.
