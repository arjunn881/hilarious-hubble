Sunscreen is one of the most frequently confiscated toiletries at TSA checkpoints — not because it's prohibited, but because travelers don't realize it counts as a liquid. Here's exactly how the rules work and how to pack sunscreen without losing it at security.

---

## TSA Sunscreen Rules Summary

- **Carry-On (Cabin):** ⚠️ Allowed — but 3.4 oz (100ml) per container maximum; must fit in quart-sized bag
- **Checked Baggage:** ✅ Allowed in any size
- **TSA Declaration Required:** No (unless container is over 3.4 oz — then it must go in checked bag)
- **Sunscreen Sticks:** ✅ Exempt from liquid rule — any size allowed in carry-on

---

## Is Sunscreen a Liquid Under TSA Rules?

**Yes.** Sunscreen in lotion, cream, spray, or gel form is classified as a liquid by TSA and is subject to the 3-1-1 liquids rule.

| Sunscreen Format | TSA Liquid Rule | Carry-On Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Lotion sunscreen** | ✅ Yes — liquid/cream | ⚠️ 3.4 oz (100ml) max |
| **Spray sunscreen (aerosol)** | ✅ Yes — aerosol | ⚠️ 3.4 oz (100ml) max |
| **Gel sunscreen** | ✅ Yes — gel | ⚠️ 3.4 oz (100ml) max |
| **Sunscreen stick (solid)** | ❌ No — solid | ✅ Any size |
| **Mineral powder sunscreen** | ❌ No — powder/solid | ✅ Any size |
| **Tinted SPF moisturizer** | ✅ Yes — liquid | ⚠️ 3.4 oz (100ml) max |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A standard full-size sunscreen bottle is 6–8 oz — too large for carry-on. Either buy a travel-size 3 oz version or decant into a reusable travel bottle before the airport.

## Best Strategy for Packing Sunscreen

### Option 1: Buy Travel-Size Sunscreen
Most major brands sell 1–3 oz travel sizes:
- **Neutrogena Ultra Sheer** — available in 3 oz
- **Coppertone Sport** — available in 2 oz
- **EltaMD UV Clear** — available in 1.7 oz
- **Banana Boat Sport** — available in 2.5 oz

### Option 2: Use Sunscreen Sticks
**Sunscreen sticks** are solid format and are **completely exempt from the TSA liquid rule**. You can bring any size in carry-on. Popular options:
- Sun Bum SPF 50 Stick
- Banana Boat Clearly Sheer Stick
- Neutrogena Sheer Zinc Stick

### Option 3: Decant Into a Reusable Travel Bottle
Buy a 3 oz silicone travel bottle and fill it from your full-size sunscreen at home. These are reusable, leak-proof, and TSA-compliant.

### Option 4: Check Your Bag
If you're going to a sunny destination and need a large supply, pack full-size sunscreen in your checked bag with no restrictions.

### Option 5: Buy at Destination
For beach or resort destinations, buying sunscreen locally is often cheaper than paying for checked baggage. Major chains (CVS, Walgreens, Walmart, international pharmacies) carry sunscreen at every resort area.

## Spray Sunscreen on a Plane — Special Rules

Aerosol sunscreen sprays follow the TSA liquids rule in carry-on AND have an additional rule in checked bags:

**Carry-On:**
- Must be 3.4 oz (100ml) or less per can
- Must fit in your quart-sized liquids bag

**Checked Bag:**
- Allowed in any quantity up to 18 oz per container and 70 oz total per passenger
- This limit applies to ALL aerosol products combined (hairspray + deodorant + sunscreen + dry shampoo)

> [!NOTE]
> The 70 oz checked bag aerosol limit applies to all aerosols combined, not just sunscreen. If you're packing multiple aerosol products, count them all toward that limit.

---

## International Flights: Sunscreen Rules

For **international flights**, sunscreen follows the same carry-on liquid rule — 100ml (3.4 oz) per container in a 1-liter (quart-sized) bag. The EU, UK, Australia, Canada, and most countries use the same 100ml liquid limit as the U.S.

**Duty-free exception:** Sunscreen purchased in an airport duty-free shop after security can be brought in its original sealed bag, even if over 100ml, for connecting flights — but rules vary by route and connecting country.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is sunscreen a liquid for TSA?**
A: Yes. Sunscreen lotion, cream, gel, and spray are all classified as liquids/aerosols and must follow the 3.4 oz carry-on limit. Sunscreen sticks are solid and exempt.

**Q: Can I bring a full-size sunscreen on a plane?**
A: Not in carry-on. Standard 6–8 oz bottles exceed the 3.4 oz limit. Pack full-size sunscreen in checked bags, or buy travel-size versions for carry-on.

**Q: What about SPF moisturizer or tinted SPF foundation?**
A: Yes — tinted SPF moisturizer, BB cream with SPF, and CC cream are all liquids and subject to the 3.4 oz carry-on limit.

**Q: Can you bring sunscreen through TSA in a checked bag?**
A: Yes. Any size sunscreen is allowed in checked bags with no TSA restriction (aerosol cans are limited to 18 oz each and 70 oz total for all aerosols).

**Q: Can I carry sunscreen spray on a plane?**
A: In carry-on: only 3.4 oz cans or smaller, in your liquids bag. In checked bags: up to 18 oz per can, 70 oz total for all aerosols.

## Conclusion

Bring sunscreen — but pack it smart. For carry-on travel, use a 3.4 oz or smaller lotion, or switch to a sunscreen stick (fully exempt from liquid rules). For beach vacations with a checked bag, pack full-size bottles with no restrictions. Don't lose your favorite SPF at the security checkpoint — switch to travel size before you leave home.
